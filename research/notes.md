## Product interpretation

The raw “put the HOA inside ChatGPT” framing is too broad and carries hallucination risk. ArcReady narrows it to one auditable job: screen a homeowner's project against versioned rules, show exact citations, and produce an architectural-review packet checklist. The HOA or management company is the buyer; the resident is the user.

## Prototype evidence policy

All community names, rules, application IDs, findings, and packet states are explicitly fictional demo fixtures. The screening is deterministic, not AI-generated. The prototype does not submit an application, contact a board, interpret city code, or provide legal advice.
